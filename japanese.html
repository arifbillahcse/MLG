Course details

What we cover, month by month

The N5 track in order. N4 continues the same structure with compound grammar, keigo basics and 200 further kanji.

MONTH 1

Hiragana & katakana

Both syllabaries written and read to automaticity, plus greetings, numbers and self-introduction. You will be writing sentences by week three.

MONTH 2

Core sentence patterns

です / ます forms, particles は・が・を・に・で, demonstratives, and the counting system Japanese actually uses day to day.

MONTH 3

Verbs & first kanji

Verb groups, te-form, past and negative. First 60 kanji introduced by radical, not by rote list.

MONTH 4

Exam drilling & kaiwa

Full JLPT N5 papers under timed conditions, listening practice at exam speed, and weekly conversation sessions with the instructor.

Every student sits at least four full mock papers before the real exam. Scores are returned with a written breakdown by section, so you know which skill to push before exam day.

After the course

Where N4 takes you

The course is a means, not the goal. Here is what it unlocks.

  1. N5

    Language school entry

    Meets the minimum most Japanese language schools require to issue an offer.

  2. N4

    Stronger visa file

    Immigration reads proficiency as evidence of genuine intent. N4 files are read differently to N5 ones.

  3. N4+

    Part-time work

    Enough Japanese to hold the interview and follow instructions in a real workplace.

  4. N2

    Japanese-taught degrees

    Universities teaching in Japanese want N2. N4 is the halfway mark, not the destination.
