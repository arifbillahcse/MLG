The Japan route

Six steps, about 14 months

Real timelines, not marketing ones. Where a step depends on intake season we say so.

  1. Month 0

    Free counselling

    Budget, target intake and university shortlist decided in one sitting.

  2. Month 1–5

    Japanese N5

    Hiragana, katakana, 800 words, basic grammar. Three classes a week.

  3. Month 6–9

    JLPT / NAT test

    Sit the exam in July or December. N5 is the minimum most schools accept.

  4. Month 9–11

    Application & COE

    School application, financial documents, Certificate of Eligibility filed with immigration.

  5. Month 12–13

    Student visa

    Embassy submission and interview preparation once the COE is issued.

  6. Month 14

    Departure

    Pre-departure briefing, accommodation confirmed, part-time work rules explained.
